Selected Projects

Selected areas of work in computational mechanics and simulation automation.

Composite Micromechanics Modeling

Abaqus

Finite element modeling of fiber-reinforced composites to study deformation and interactions at the microscale. This work includes representative volume elements, periodic boundary conditions, and nonlinear contact.

Simulation Preparation and Diagnostics

Python / Abaqus

Python tools for preparing finite element models and checking mesh connectivity, boundary conditions, and contact definitions. Simulation diagnostics help identify numerical issues and assess the consistency of results.

Simulation Data and Visualization

Python / HPC

Workflows for running simulations on high-performance computing systems, extracting field and history outputs, and preparing data for visualization. The goal is to make simulation results easier to inspect and compare.

Soft Body Armor Modeling

Prior work / ASU

My master's research at Arizona State University explored a framework for soft body armor design using solid finite elements. This earlier work forms part of my background in composite modeling and impact mechanics.
